# Bourbon based cocktails

## Boulevardier

- 1 1/2 oz bourbon
- 3/4 oz sweet vermouth
- 3/4 oz campari
- lemon twist

## New York Sour

Shake everything (except red wine) with ice, add red wine as floater with spoon

- 1 1/2 oz bourbon
- 3/4 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz demerara syrup
- Egg white
- 3/4 oz red wine

## Old Fashioned

- 2 oz bourbon
- 1/2 oz demerara syrup
- 4 dashes angostura bitters
- orange peel

## Paper Plane

- 3/4 oz bourbon
- 3/4 oz aperol
- 3/4 oz amaro nonino
- 1/4 oz lemmon juice
